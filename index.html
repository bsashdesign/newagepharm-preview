        Special orders

        Ask us about any product you need. If a product isn’t in stock, we can usually order it for you and deliver it for free.

        • Ask us: call, send a WhatsApp, or tell us in the store. The product’s name or a photo of it helps.
        • We check with our suppliers and let you know if we can get it, and when.
        • When it’s in, pick it up, or we’ll deliver it for free in our same-day area.
