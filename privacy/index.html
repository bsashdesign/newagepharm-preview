              Privacy

              How the New Age Pharmacy website handles your information.

              What you type stays in your browser

              The product search, the delivery checker and the "find your plan" box all run in your browser. What you type isn't sent to us or saved.

              Visit counts

              We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, which pages are viewed and which websites sent visitors here. It sets no cookies, doesn't identify you and doesn't follow you to other websites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

              We also count taps on buttons such as Call, WhatsApp and Refill, and whether a delivery check or plan search found a match. We don't keep the ZIP code, plan name or anything else you typed.

              Server logs

              This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare briefly keeps standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

              We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell or share your information.

              Messages and forms on other services

              Our WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a message already typed in. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the message is then handled by WhatsApp under its privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

              Refill and transfer requests use forms on Good Neighbor Pharmacy's website (mygnp.com), which send your request to us. Those forms follow its privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

              Directions open Google Maps, which follows Google's privacy policy.

              Your health information

              Medicine requests open WhatsApp with the medicine, strength and quantity you picked, and nothing is sent until you press send. Messages on WhatsApp aren't part of your pharmacy record. To keep a request private, call us at (718) 253-1700. Please don't include other health details in messages.

              As a pharmacy, we protect your health information as federal law (HIPAA) requires. Ask us at the counter for a copy of our Notice of Privacy Practices.

              Questions

              Call us at (718) 253-1700, email newagepharmacy@verizon.net, or stop by at 2918 Avenue J. See also our terms of use.
