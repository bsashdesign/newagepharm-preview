    Outside our delivery area
    1. Find your prescription label

      It’s on your bottle, box or package. You’ll enter these in the secure transfer form:

      1. 1Your current pharmacy’s name
      2. 2The medication’s name
      3. 3The Rx # (prescription number)
      • Use the label from your most recent fill.
      • Transferring more than one? You can add them all in the same form.
    2. Request your transfer

      Enter the details from your label in our secure transfer form.

      For delivery: ask for it in the Notes field and tell us which day works best. New Age will call if they need your address, or a payment method for a co-pay.

      Picking it up? Add any timing you’d like in the Notes field.
