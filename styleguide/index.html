Picture cards

How a card with a picture is put together (PromoCard.astro, Shaped.astro, base.css .shaped), drawn to scale for a card 372 px wide (a 412 px phone), then the real cards.

  1. The card crops everything in it at its rounded edge: the shape, the backdrop and the subject. Nothing else cuts them.
  2. The picture’s side is 46% of the card on a phone (38% for a compact card; 43% from 640 px up), the card’s full height, on the right, or on the left (mirrored: the shape and backdrop, never the picture).
  3. The box is 9 wide by 10 tall. It’s as tall as the card, but never more than 1.44 × the side’s width, so at most 30% wider than its side (--box-h: min(100cqh, 144cqw)). It stands on the card’s bottom, against its outer edge: when it’s wider than its side, the card’s outer edge crops it.
  4. The shape is placed in the box’s units: its center and size in % of the box’s width (cqw) or height (cqh). A circle by its curve (data-curve: corner, arch, diagonal…), a star nearly as tall as the box (96cqh) with its right point just inside. So a shape looks the same on a card and on a page head, only bigger or smaller.
  5. The fill can reach past the box, left and up, by 150% of the box’s width (60% and sideways only when a backdrop photo fills it), so a huge circle’s curve can come in from far away and run under the words. The card’s edges crop it.
  6. The subject: a product stands centered on the box’s bottom, sinking 6% past it, its top 3% under the box’s top (object-fit: contain, so it’s whole). A person cut off at the bottom (cutBottom) is drawn 130% wide and sinks 14%, so the card’s bottom hides the cut. The eye chart stands against the right edge (align="right").
  7. On a page head the box is the picture column’s width (40% of the band), 9:10, at the band’s top, its shape staying in that column.

In each frame: the card as it shows, its parts outlined, then the same card with nothing cropped (dimmed where the card cuts it away). A red line is a straight cut inside the card: something other than the card’s own edge cropping the shape or the subject (the image guide: never an edge inside the card). The table under each card has the numbers for the three widths; a yellow cell is something to look at.

Special orders

Ask us about any product you need. If a product isn’t in stock, we can usually order it for you and deliver it for free.

  • Ask us: call, send a WhatsApp, or tell us in the store. The product’s name or a photo of it helps.
  • We check with our suppliers and let you know if we can get it, and when.
  • When it’s in, pick it up, or we’ll deliver it for free in our same-day area.

A listing doesn’t mean New Age Pharmacy stocks or can fill this medication. Please call us to confirm.
